# Scoring

## Per competency (0 to 10)

Score each competency from the plan on the evidence in the answers. One weak answer is
not a 2 if a follow-up recovered it; judge the best evidence and the overall pattern.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Exceptional for the level. Concrete, measured, insightful. Rare. |
| 7–8 | Clearly meets the bar. Would pass this round. |
| 5–6 | Borderline. Some signal, but gaps a real interviewer would notice. |
| 3–4 | Below the bar. Vague, generic or partly wrong. |
| 0–2 | No signal, wrong, or not answered. |

## Overall score

1. Compute the weighted average of the competency scores.
2. Adjust it by at most ±0.5 for things that cut across competencies (below).
3. Round **down** to one decimal (6.48 → 6.4). Never round up.

Interview stopped early ("stop"): score only the lines you have evidence for, mark the
others "not assessed", and spread their weight over the assessed lines. Say so in the report.

Adjustments:
- − answers consistently too long or too short, rambling, contradictions, bad attitude;
- + clear structure, honest about limits, good questions at the end.

## Calibration

Calibrate to the seniority the user gave. A junior is not judged against a senior bar.

- 5.0 is "maybe", not "average". Most real candidates land between 4 and 7.
- Do not give 8+ unless you would defend the hire in a debrief.
- Never round up to be kind. A 6.48 is a 6.4.

## Verdict

| Overall | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 8.0+ | Strong hire |
| 7.0–7.9 | Hire |
| 5.5–6.9 | Borderline: depends on the other candidates |
| < 5.5 | No hire |

Cap: if a competency with a weight of 20% or more scores 3 or below, the verdict label is
at most "Borderline", whatever the score. Keep the score as computed and add one line in the
report explaining the cap.

## Red flags (always reported, whatever the score)

- Badmouthing a former employer or colleague.
- Claims that contradict the CV or each other.
- Not knowing what the company does, when the user gave you the company name.
- No questions at all for the interviewer. (Questions only about logistics, like "what are
  the next steps?", are not a red flag but count as weak in the report.)
